The marks covered are the name **CaptainPBX**, the product names **Captain Core, Captain Shield, Captain Trust, Captain Bridge, Captain Watch, Captain Vault, Captain Bulk, Captain AI, Captain Auth, Captain Audit, Captain Remind and Captain Code**, the CaptainPBX logos and wordmark, and the **captainpbx.com** domain.

## You may

- Run, study, change and share the software under the license.
- Keep the "Powered by CaptainPBX" attribution, as the license requires.
- Say factually that your product or service is "based on CaptainPBX", "a fork of CaptainPBX", "compatible with CaptainPBX", or that you offer "support for CaptainPBX".
- Use the name in articles, tutorials, talks and reviews.
- Redistribute unmodified official releases under the CaptainPBX name.

## You may not

- Call a modified version, a fork, a hosted or managed service, or an appliance or installer image "CaptainPBX" or any of the product names above, or use a confusingly similar name.
- Remove or hide the "Powered by CaptainPBX" attribution, or replace it with your own branding while keeping the software otherwise the same (white labelling).
- Use the logos in your product, website or marketing, except to link to the project.
- Suggest that the CaptainPBX project sponsors, endorses or certifies your product or service.
- Register domains, company names, app store listings or social media accounts that contain the marks or are confusingly similar.

## Forks

If you publish a modified version, give it a different name and logo, mark it as modified, and keep the attribution and the license notices. That is all it takes.
