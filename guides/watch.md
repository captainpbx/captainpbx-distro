# Captain Watch

**Home** menu. System view and inside an organization.

Captain Watch is the first place to look when someone says the phones are down. It shows the live state, not a settings form.

## Do this

1. Open **Home → Captain Watch**. Inside an organization the label is **Watch**.
2. Look for phones, calls, and services that are not healthy.
3. If one phone is the problem, open **EndpointWatch** next.

## You are done when

You can tell whether the problem is the whole appliance or one phone.
