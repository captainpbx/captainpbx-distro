# Activity

**Captain AI** menu. Inside an organization.

This page shows activity. The switch that turns Captain AI on for the appliance is under **System → Captain AI**.

## Do this

1. Select the organization.
2. Open **Captain AI → Activity**.
3. Read the recent work. If the list stays empty, confirm Captain AI is enabled on the system page and that voicemail or call recording is allowed to be transcribed.

## You are done when

Work you expected, such as a voicemail transcription, shows here after it finishes.
