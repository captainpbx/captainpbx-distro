# Business Hours

**Voice** menu. Inside an organization.

A schedule does nothing by itself. You attach it where calls arrive, usually an inbound route or an IVR.

## Do this

1. Open **Voice → Business Hours**.
2. Add a schedule, name it, and set the days and hours you are open.
3. Save, then run **Sync & Apply**.
4. On the inbound route or IVR, choose this schedule for the open path and another destination for the closed path.

## You are done when

A call during open hours follows the open destination. A call outside those hours follows the closed one.
