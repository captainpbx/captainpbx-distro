# People

**People** menu. System view and inside an organization.

In the system view you see people across organizations. Inside an organization you see the people who belong there. Create the web login here. Create the phone number under Extensions.

## Do this

1. Open **People → People**.
2. Add the person, their username, and a password.
3. Choose the organization they belong to and the role they should have.
4. If they also need a phone, create or attach their extension under **Voice → Extensions**.

## You are done when

They can sign in to the web with that username. Their menu matches the role you gave them.
