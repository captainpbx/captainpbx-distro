# Captain Bridge

**System** menu. System view.

Use Bridge when the phone system is fine and the computer under it needs a change. Everyday extensions and routes are not here.

## Do this

1. Open **System → Captain Bridge**.
2. Open the task you need, such as the network address.
3. Change it and apply. If you change the address you are using, reconnect on the new address.

## You are done when

The appliance answers on the address you set.
