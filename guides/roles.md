# Roles & Permissions

**Platform** menu. System view.

You assign a role to a person on the People page. You edit what that role means here. A view-only role can look and cannot save.

## Do this

1. Stay in the system view.
2. Open **Platform → Roles & Permissions**.
3. Open a role and tick what it may do. Leave save and delete off for a person who should only look.
4. Save, then assign that role to the person.

## You are done when

A person with that role sees only the menus you allowed, and cannot save when the role is view-only.
