# Teams

**People** menu. Inside an organization.

Use teams to group people. To control what someone can open, use their role on the People page, not the team.

## Do this

1. Open **People → Teams**.
2. Add a team and give it a clear name.
3. Add the people who belong in it.
4. Save.

## You are done when

The team lists the people you added, and those people show the team on their record.
