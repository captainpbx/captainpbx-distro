# Captain AI

**System** menu. System view.

This is the switch for the whole box. Each organization then sees its own work under Captain AI → Activity.

## Do this

1. Open **System → Captain AI**.
2. Enable it and set the options you want, such as voicemail or call recordings.
3. Save.
4. Leave a test voicemail, then look under the organization's **Activity** page.

## You are done when

A finished transcription shows on the organization's Activity page.
