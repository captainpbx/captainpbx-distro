# Calling Policy

**Voice** menu. Inside an organization.

Use a policy when a phone may call inside the organization but should not dial outside, or the other way around.

## Do this

1. Open **Voice → Calling Policy** and add a policy.
2. Choose what that policy allows.
3. Assign the policy to the extensions that should follow it.
4. Save, then run **Sync & Apply**.

## You are done when

A restricted extension is refused when it dials a destination the policy does not allow. Other extensions are unchanged.
